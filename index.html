Traffic Flow Optimiser

Upload junction footage to estimate vehicle counts by direction and preview an adaptive signal plan.

How to use Traffic Flow Optimiser

1 · Add footage

Use the sample junction photo, or choose a photo or video from this device.

2 · Check the counts

Vehicle detection shows the total and estimates the active approaches. Correct counts if needed.

3 · See the signal plan

Detected vehicles are grouped into up to eight compass directions. The largest queue goes first.

4 · Play the clearing

One approach gets green at a time. After its queue clears, the next busiest approach moves.

Use Reset cars to replay after the vehicles clear. This is a virtual prototype and does not control real traffic signals.
